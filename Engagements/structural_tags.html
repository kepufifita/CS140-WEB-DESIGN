What goes in the Header?

Header shows the brand name, and has the navigation in some cases

What goes in the Nav?

Nav goes under the header, and sometimes inside the header. It has a search bar, cart if website is a shopping website, and filters

What goes in Main?

Main puts words in a specific area of the website

What goes into the Footer?

Links to other websites, Copyright,Privacy Policy Legal Notices, Cookie Preferences, Links to social media webpages,Contact information, etc.
