What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?

The block tags have room to share the same line on a webpage but don't. It creates a box and we can set the height, width, border, margin, and padding of the box. Inline tags share te same line on a webpage.

What is the margin, border, and padding?

The margin is the outside of the box. We add to the margin to move stuff away from other things from the outside. The padding is the inside of the box. We add to the padding to move things away from the inside.
