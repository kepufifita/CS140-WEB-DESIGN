My favorite Hero of all time has got to be Izuku Midoriya from the anime My Hero Academia. I think that Izuku Midoriya, or "Deku" is a great example of a hero. Despite him growing up without a "quirk," or superpower, he continually ran forward to help others. His story catches the attention of the top hero and he ends up becoming his successor and does his best to learn and hone this new power he gains. One thing I had learned from this character is that it is ok to ask for help when needed! I also learned that though life may be hard or unfair sometimes, we must keep moving forward and keep doing our best! GO PLUS ULTRA!!

Izuku Midoriya "Deku"
Midoriya went by "Deku," in middle school because he was the only one in class, it means useless. Now that he has made it into UA high school, His hero name is Deku because it means much more than what they would call him in middle school. To him it means "You can do it!"
Quirk: One for All
One for all is a power passed down from All-Might, his mentor and former number one hero! Since his retirement, Deku has been doing his best to hone this power and make it his own!
