Marvel Rivals

Interests Page Hero page

One my favorite things to do is to play video games. One video game that I enjoy playing at the moment is a game called Marvel Rivals. Marvel rivals is a third person hero shooter that brings all of your favorite Marvel superheroes together and battle to save the time stream. The play style is similar to Overwatch, but has it's own unique ways to play as well.

As of right now, there are 54 characters to choose from, and they are all free to play!When this game was announced, I was so excited because I love anything Marvel! There are so many characters that aren't even in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, that are finally getting the opportunity to debut in this game!
