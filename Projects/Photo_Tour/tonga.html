Tonga is the motherland! I am so proud that my family is from the beautiful island kingdom! The last time I was in tonga, I was such a small baby visiting my dad's family in Ha'ateiho. It would make my heart full if I had the opportunity to visit where my ancestors lived and grew up.

In Tonga, I would like to visit:

  • Nukualofa
  • Ha'ateiho
  • Matahau

I want to visit Nukualofa because it is one of the 3 main islands in tonga. I want to visit Ha'ateiho because that is where my dad grew up, and finally, I want to visit Matahau because that is where my grandparents grew up! I believe family history is so important, and being able to go back to tonga and learn about my family would be such a blessing to me
